Human Reasoning under Plural Assistant Support
Mira Halvorsen
Tobias Renner
We study how people solve mathematics problems and write essays when several language models help them at the same time. Participants who consulted multiple assistants compared the answers and relied less on any single one. Open-ended writing became more varied, while arithmetic accuracy stayed the same.
